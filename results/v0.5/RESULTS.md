# v0.5 pilot results — uncalibrated spacecraft simulator

Experiments completed locally using fixed and separate training/calibration/evaluation seeds. These are **illustrative research pilot numbers** and not spacecraft verification evidence.

## Monitoring (40 fault and 16 healthy-control campaigns; 30 simulated min each)

| Monitor | Fault scenarios warned during active episode | False alert episodes per healthy simulated hour | Conditional median warning delay |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Requirement limits | 45% | 0.000 | 480 s |
| Residual slope | 40% | 0.000 | 90 s |
| CUSUM | 40% | 0.000 | 270 s |
| Physics residual / sensor disagreement | 60% | 0.000 | 300 s |
| Isolation Forest | 0% | 0.250 | Not detected |
| Supervised gradient boosting | 100% | 0.125 | 150 s |

**Interpretation:** The gradient booster was trained on other runs generated by **the same five hand-designed fault mechanisms** and therefore does not establish generalization to actual spacecraft faults. Isolation Forest performed poorly at our separately healthy-calibrated threshold. Positive warnings are not confirmed verification failures. Delay is conditional on detection and should not be compared without considering detection rate.

## Active verification comparison (20 fault and 8 healthy-control campaigns per policy)

All methods use the same 16-test catalog, 35 simulated-minute budget, and a fault onset of 10 simulated minutes.

| Verification policy | Fault-case detection | Healthy false failure |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fixed | 40% | 0% |
| Random | 35% | 0% |
| Model-fixed | 60% | 0% |
| Adaptive one-pass | 40% | 0% |
| Scheduled retesting | 60% | 0% |
| Temporal-blind | 40% | 0% |
| Temporal-adaptive | 80% | 0% |

**Interpretation:** Not statistically conclusive (only four seeds across five synthetic fault families). The policies change the spacecraft trajectory through the tests they choose, so the controlled comparison uses identical initial conditions and fault schedules, not identical physical trajectories.

## What's not yet implemented

- Full ADCS, multi-node thermal, high-fidelity battery electrochemistry, validated flight data.
- Trained ML-based *test selection* and Bayesian active diagnosis.
- Calibrated probability of fault or time to a future voltage-limit violation.
- Generalization to entirely unseen fault mechanisms.
