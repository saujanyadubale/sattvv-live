# v0.9.3 integrated five-stage workflow: methods and validity

## Scope
This is a **simulation-based research demonstrator**. It is not a spacecraft digital twin validated against flight data, not an ECSS qualification system, and not real EGSE. Historical v0.1–v0.9.1 APIs and legacy UI are preserved.

### Closed-loop decision order
At each test-selection instant on one persistent coupled plant:
1. **Detect:** obtain only telemetry already available; update causal rolling features and chosen threshold, physics-residual, CUSUM, trend, or ML anomaly detector. These are *warnings*, not failures.
2. **Predict:** generate horizon-specific voltage and temperature forecasts using an offline-trained gradient-boosted model and independently calibrated campaign-max split-conformal bands. Physics extrapolation and linear trend forecasts are also returned. A forecasted crossing is *not* a requirement FAIL. Coverage assumptions require exchangeability and do not hold automatically under distribution shift.
3. **Diagnose:** maintain Bayesian test-outcome hypotheses over nine known single-fault mechanisms, using offline synthetic likelihood tables. The posterior is updated after PASS/FAIL checks, not from the hidden simulator fault label. Composite faults are **not** represented as root-cause hypotheses, and reported weights are not calibrated diagnostic confidence.
4. **Verify:** choose a legal, fitting, time-budget-compliant procedure from T01–T20. The `predictive_active` score combines requirement novelty, evidence freshness, test criticality, sensor warnings, possible future voltage/temperature limit crossing, expected information gain, repeat penalty and duration. Tests T07/T13/T14 are excluded at low SOC, low current bus voltage or forecast *point* crossing; T06/T15 are excluded at high temperature. These are illustrative operational guards, not formal safety certification.
5. **Audit:** independently recompute structural PASS/FAIL status from each procedure's requirement checks, verify test-catalog coverage, timestamp continuity and budget. This catches report inconsistencies, **not** flaws in the underlying physics or in the correctness of the requirements.

### Matched policies
The new pipeline evaluates `fixed`, `temporal_adaptive`, `information_gain`, `learned_risk`, and `predictive_active`. Every policy receives the same type of causal observation and identical forecasting/monitoring interfaces. Only `predictive_active` uses forecasts in its test-selection utility. Each policy runs on a distinct initialized spacecraft (its own subsequent physical trajectory depends on commanded tests). Policy training uses predefined synthetic seeds; held-out compound faults are assessed separately.

### Benchmark and failure metrics
`python -m satvv bench-pipeline --seeds 2` evaluates seven stress families with two seeds each at budgets 20, 25, and 35 min. Reports include faulty-case detection from **executed failed requirements**, healthy-case false-failure rate, elapsed simulated time and procedure count. This is a small *pilot* (10 faulty + 4 healthy campaigns per policy/budget). Results must not be presented as statistically established superiority. For rigorous future work, test many more independent fault families and seeds and report family-clustered uncertainty intervals.

### Important limitations
- All synthetic mechanisms remain designed around the same simplified physical simulator.
- Known single-fault likelihood models cannot correctly represent held-out combinations.
- Trend and forecast predictive intervals are calibrated on simulated campaigns; under scenario distribution shifts, coverage may be worse than nominal.
- Some procedural tests necessarily perturb system loads and temperature; no identical time series across different test policies.
- The audit does not establish physical correctness, ground-truth root cause, safe autonomous execution, or flight readiness.
- No online LLM, reinforcement learning, or onboard deployed system is present.

### Usability
The home page organizes tasks into Experiment / Compare methods / Research & evidence with full scientifically implemented controls under Advanced settings. Stress-case severity and eclipse are intentionally randomized by a fixed seed and those controls are disabled. Historical experiment tabs remain reachable at `/legacy`.
