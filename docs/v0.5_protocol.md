# v0.5 experimental protocol — preprint-style pilot

## Research questions

**RQ1**: Can residual-based and ML monitoring warn during developing or recurring faults before any *executed* requirement check fails?

**RQ2**: Under a fixed simulated time budget, can the seven test-selection policies cover the expanded 16-test catalog and detect faults that occur after verification begins?

**RQ3**: Can these methods avoid warnings/failures during healthy solar/eclipses and transient-load operation?

No claim of technological novelty or spacecraft-validated performance is made.

## Study design

**Truth mechanism:** scripted scenario name, `Episode(mechanism,start,end,severity,ramp)`; scenario metadata and labels are held only in the evaluation harness. Observed fields include voltage, current, temperatures, SOC estimate, load, eclipse-affected solar generation, operating mode, and communications metrics. Hidden battery internal resistance and fault schedule are not passed to policies.

**Paired environments:** Each verification policy gets the same seed, scenario definition, onset, eclipse flag, initial SOC and budget, but executes different test actions and therefore may change the physical state trajectory. This reflects genuine policy-action coupling; it does not guarantee identical counterfactual trajectories.

**Synthetic training and evaluation:**
- ML training seeds `100..104` by default (5 seeds, seven scenario variants each).
- Healthy-only calibration seeds `500..502`.
- Evaluation seeds `800..803` (4 seeds), crossing five injected fault families and two healthy controls, with eclipse on/off.
- Training faults use onset 360 s, severity 0.85; evaluation varies onset 420–540 s and severity 1.0–1.2.
- The classifier is tested on disjoint campaigns, **but not disjoint fault mechanisms**. Because physics and simulator parameters are shared, this is in-family synthetic generalization only.

**Verification benchmark:** seeds `1000..1003`, onset 180 or 600 seconds, budgets 20 and 35 minutes, seven policies, five faulty plus two control scenarios. Two control families include healthy nominal and an intentionally non-fault load transient. Synthetic time advances in 10-second steps during tests and 60-second waits.

## Primary outcomes

- **Monitoring fault detection fraction**: fraction of faulty campaigns with a debounced warning episode **while the injected fault is active** (not a diagnostic accuracy score).
- **Healthy false-alert episodes per simulated hour**: alert episodes in healthy or transient-load control campaigns divided by healthy total duration in hours.
- **Median warning delay**: median first in-episode warning time minus first fault onset, among detected campaigns only. Not a lead-time-to-failure estimate.
- **Formal test detection fraction**: fraction of faulty campaigns with a **completed executed verification check** returning `FAIL` before budget expiration.
- **False verification failure fraction**: fraction of healthy control campaigns with a completed failed verification requirement.

Policy evidence is marked PASS, FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE, or WAIT (the last is not a test). All methods stop after the first failed requirement check. Healthy-control zero failures in a small pilot does not prove a zero failure rate.

## Comparators

1. Requirement limit monitoring.
2. Sliding-window OLS trend of physics residual.
3. CUSUM of physics residual.
4. Direct physics-based residual threshold (+sensor disagreement).
5. Isolation Forest on 12 engineered telemetry features.
6. Supervised histogram gradient boosting classifier on the same features.

Seven test policies: fixed, random, model-fixed, adaptive, scheduled-retest, temporal-blind, temporal-adaptive. All have access to the **same 16 test definitions** and budgets. Fixed and random scan ahead for a test that fits the remaining time; model-fixed dynamically scores untested cases without telemetry risk; temporal policies may repeat executed tests. Unlike v0.4, the new simulator changes the physical dynamics, so result magnitudes must be analyzed within this version only.

## Falsification and ablations to add

- Completely unseen fault mechanisms, rather than merely new seeds.
- Wider uncertainty conditions: drifting measurement bias, missing packets, sensor update rate, stale state estimates.
- Higher-dimensional interacting faults with independently varied configuration parameters.
- Verify detector tuning exclusively on training/calibration data and compare at matched healthy false-alert rate.
- Compare scheduler cost under unobserved predictive uncertainty and under safety constraints.
- Bootstrap by **scenario group**, not individual adjacent time samples; evaluate 30+ independently varied campaigns before strong claims.

## Reference implementation caveat

`REQ-EPS-I` is described as `2.60 A` in the historical v0.1 static test catalog but dynamically tested using the `1.30 A` limit introduced in v0.2. This must be reconciled by explicit versioned requirements before the traceability matrix can be treated as a production verification specification. The new T13–T16 requirements are stored separately in `verification_v05.EXTRA_REQUIREMENTS`.
