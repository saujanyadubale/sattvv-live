This release **does not run external tools**, benchmark their performance, or inspect proprietary implementations. The website graph describes publicly documented **focus and role**, not an exhaustive feature checklist or ranking.

## Reading the capability matrix

- **Documented focus** (●): central stated purpose in the cited source.
- **Related capability/scope** (◐): described as adjacent or enabled, but not the emphasized reason this source is included.
- **Not assessed** (·): the official page above was not used to verify that capability; **never interpret as absence**.
- **Prototype implementation** (◆): included in our Python code; **not independently validated against physical flight hardware**.

Both axes are analyst-selected conceptual dimensions, not externally agreed standard ratings. No claims that NOS3, COSMOS, ADAPT or HyDE cannot do active test scheduling should be inferred from this table. No claim of novelty is established by it.

## What our contribution is and isn't

Research objective: examine whether predictive and diagnostic information changes *which verification procedure should be run next* under a limited budget, compared with fixed, temporal and learned baselines.

The integration itself is not an established new algorithm. Demonstrating research merit requires independent models, unseen faults, calibrated forecasts and uncertainty, external datasets for passive detection, and ideally hardware-based experiments for active testing. **Real OPS-SAT-AD recordings cannot answer counterfactual questions about tests that were never executed.**

### Existing internal pilot benchmark (not updated in v0.9.5)

See `results/v0.9.4/RESULTS.md`. Internal verification strategies can be fairly compared under specified paired synthetic scenarios, although results remain sensitive to scenario design and are not transferable to real satellites without validation. Do **not** place the resulting fault-detection percentages next to externally published NOS3/OPS-SAT data as though they were head-to-head metrics.
