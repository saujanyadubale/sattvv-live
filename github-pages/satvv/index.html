02 / INSIDE THE SYSTEM

Five decisions. One loop.

Not every component is machine learning. Physics and statistical checks, trained models, Bayesian updates and decision policies each play a distinct role.

Detect

Threshold checks, persistence-tested weak-signal residuals, CUSUM, Isolation Forest and gradient boosting surface unusual behavior; warnings never equal verified failures.

Predict

Physical baselines and gradient-boosted regression estimate future voltage and temperature with synthetic uncertainty bands.

Diagnose

Bayesian hypothesis updates consider requirement-level evidence. They rank possible explanations, not confirmed causes.

Choose the test

Fixed, temporal, information-gain, learned-ranking and predictive-active policies decide which procedure to execute next.

Audit

Recorded PASS/FAIL outcomes, limits, timestamps and policy provenance are checked for internal consistency.

SIMULATOR → TELEMETRY → DETECT + PREDICT → DIAGNOSE → SELECT + VERIFY ↺

Our research question

Can future-risk estimates improve what we test next?

Not consistently in these pilots. The learned-ranking and information-gain methods can outperform the integrated predictive-active approach at different budgets.

We report weak faults, missed detections and uncertain diagnoses alongside successes.

Internal software tests and synthetic fault campaigns do not demonstrate real spacecraft reliability.
