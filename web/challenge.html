GLB import requirements and NASA model download guidance

Upload one local binary glTF 2.0 (.glb) file, at most 30 MB. Include mesh geometry and embed textures in the GLB (external .gltf/.bin and remote textures are unsupported). The loader normalizes your model to fit the scene. Centered geometry and reasonably aligned axes give the best results. For complex models use Blender to export glTF Binary (.glb), apply transforms, and reduce polygon count. A valid file is not required to have our six named subsystem meshes: SAT-V&V overlays conceptual regions, so markers do not automatically attach to actual internal components. No uploaded geometry leaves your browser; an invalid file leaves the current model unchanged. If the model fails, check the status message and try a simple GLB.

If the high-fidelity renderer cannot load, the built-in schematic remains available. Imported NASA GLB geometry is a visual reference, not the physical architecture of our simulated craft; subsystem marker positions are conceptual. No flight CAD, orbital propagation, physical fault localization or live RF link is claimed. NASA ICECube 3D model source ↗ Injected ground truth stays hidden until revealed after the run.
