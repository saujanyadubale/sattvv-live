03 / OUR EXPERIMENTAL CONTRIBUTION

04 / METHOD-TO-METHOD COMPARISON

Where our algorithms differ from established approaches

Published tools serve distinct purposes. Below we compare the actual algorithmic approach rather than assigning arbitrary product scores.

Published or established approachTypical analytical decisionImplemented SAT-V&V analogueDifference we test
Fixed scripted verification / threshold monitorsRun predetermined procedures; check limitsFixed-order policy and requirement PASS/FAILCan evidence-dependent scheduling detect verified failures earlier under the same budget?
Change detection / residual monitoringWarn about statistical or model-based deviationsCUSUM, trend, residuals and synthetic-data ML monitorsDoes a warning improve the choice of the next procedure?
NASA ADAPT / HyDE diagnostic researchEstimate fault hypotheses from hybrid or probabilistic modelsExpert-defined Bayesian requirement-likelihood tableDoes test outcome information help allocate limited verification time?
OPS-SAT-AD anomaly-detection benchmarksEvaluate detectors on a common real telemetry datasetIsolation Forest / gradient boosting on synthetic spacecraftExternal real-data benchmarking remains outstanding; no numerical equivalence is implied.
Information-theoretic experimental designChoose observations/tests with high expected information gainBayesian information-gain and predictive-active schedulersCompare against temporal and ML rankers under paired synthetic campaigns.

05 / REPRODUCIBLE WITHIN-SIMULATOR BENCHMARK

Actual performance comparison on a shared testbed

All five implemented policies were evaluated on the same 18 synthetic faulty campaigns per budget. These numbers come from our archived v0.9.4 pilot, not tests run on NASA/ESA datasets or infrastructure.

Higher fraction = more faulty campaigns with a failed check found.

Benchmark scope: 18 faulty campaigns per strategy and budget (six fault families × three seeds); six healthy campaigns per budget. All strategies missed the heater-fault cases in this pilot. Results do not establish superiority on real spacecraft and should not be compared numerically with separately published studies.

Reproduce with python -m satvv bench-audit --seeds 3 · Source: results/v0.9.4/RESULTS.md.

Fair-comparison rule

Published results from NOS3, COSMOS, ADAPT, HyDE or OPS-SAT-AD are not directly comparable to our synthetic detection percentages. Only controlled algorithms tested on a common dataset, protocol and metric can be ranked fairly.
