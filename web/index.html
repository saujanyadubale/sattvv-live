Technical reference & evidence

For a guided explanation, visit How it works; for a graphic comparison with external systems visit Industry & research.

Behind the simplified controls, the lab runs a reproducible 20-test simulator with coupled EPS, thermal and simplified ADCS systems. Results are synthetic and not flight-qualified.

Detect

Limit checks, trends, CUSUM, physical residuals and learned anomaly detection.

Predict

Persistence, physics extrapolation, linear regression and gradient-boosted forecasting. Bands estimated from synthetic calibration data.

Diagnose

Bayesian fault hypotheses from test outcome likelihoods, not proof of the physical root cause.

Verify

Fixed, temporal-adaptive, information-gain and ML-ranked test selection.

Audit

Deterministic evidence consistency checks, distinct from machine-learning confidence.

Limitations

No real hardware or flight verification. Limited training fault families and simplified physical models.
